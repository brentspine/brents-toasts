# 2.1.4 - 2026-07-23

## Added

- Timer control API: `pauseToastTimer()`, `resumeToastTimer()`, `resetToastTimer()`, `extendToastTimer()`, and `removeToastTimer()` methods for pausing, resuming, resetting, extending, or cancelling a toast's auto-dismiss countdown by its id.
- `pauseOnHover` option (defaults `true`) to pause auto-dismiss timer on hover and resume on mouseleave; no effect on sticky toasts.
- `confirmButton()` now resets the toast timer on every click, preventing the toast from timing out while showing a confirmation prompt.

## Changed

- `confirmButton()` now calls `resetToastTimer()` internally to ensure the toast doesn't auto-dismiss while mid-confirmation.
