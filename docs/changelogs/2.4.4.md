# 2.4.4 - 2026-08-03

## Added

- `transition` option for `updateToast` and `promise()` — pass `transition: true` alongside visual changes (message, color, buttons, etc.) to crossfade into them instead of swapping instantly. The toast fades out, the update applies, then fades back in. No-op at toast creation since there's nothing to transition from yet.
- `withTransition()` builder method on `ToastBuilder` for shape-compatibility with `ToastOptions`.
