/* Reserves the same 15.5px the close bar gains on hover (4.5px -> 20px) as
   trailing space after the message instead — so a closable toast's overall
   width never changes on hover, without leaving a dead gap between the
   accent bar and the text (where they're meant to sit flush). Sits between
   .bt-toast-content and .bt-toast-actions when there are buttons, or after

   the two motions cancel out instead of one of them ever widening the row. */
